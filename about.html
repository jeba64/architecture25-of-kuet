    KUET Architecture Batch 2025 is the newest cohort of the Department of Architecture at Khulna University of Engineering & Technology — one of the leading technical universities in Bangladesh.

    This website serves as our collective digital archive — a space to document our studio work, academic projects, and shared experiences throughout our five-year journey in architectural education.

    The Department

    The Department of Architecture at KUET offers a rigorous five-year B.Arch program combining technical knowledge with creative design thinking. Students engage with studios, workshops, site visits, and interdisciplinary courses that prepare them for professional architectural practice.

    The department maintains strong ties with the architectural community of Bangladesh and fosters a culture of critical inquiry, design excellence, and social responsibility in the built environment.

    This Website

    Built entirely by students of Batch 2025, this platform is an ongoing project — growing alongside us as we progress through our academic years. Media, projects, and student profiles will be updated each semester.
